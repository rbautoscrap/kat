01 · Why this platform
Differentiation

Built for serious buyers

KOREA AUTO TRADE is designed to reduce friction in cross-border vehicle trading — with live inventory, clear listing data, and a guided purchase flow.

Real-time listing updates

New units are published continuously across Hot Deals, Car Listings, Live Auction, and Stand by. Category pages refresh order so buyers see relevant stock — not only the newest upload every time.

Transparent listing information

Each vehicle page presents structured specs (VIN, transmission, odometer, fuel), condition notes, photo galleries, and video when available — so decisions start from facts, not guesswork.

Member offer system

Registered members can submit purchase offers on listings and track them under My offers. This creates a cleaner negotiation trail than scattered chat messages alone.

Guided buying process

From price check to deposit and shipment, the How to buy flow explains currency options, documents, and shipping responsibility in four clear steps.

What this means for you Faster shortlisting, fewer repeated questions, and a professional process that scales for overseas dealers sourcing from Korea.

03 · Inventory & listing pages
Real-time stock

Find and evaluate units

Use search and category pages to shortlist vehicles, then open a listing for full detail.

Search

The top search bar supports make, model, VIN, serial number (S/N), and notes — useful when you already know a unit reference.

Sale status

Listings may show status such as on sale, reserved, or sold. Reserved / sold units follow platform rules for viewing and offers.

On every listing detail page

  • Vehicle identity — title, year / make / model, S/N
  • Key specs — VIN, engine mark, transmission, odometer, fuel type
  • Condition notes — English notes when available
  • Media — photo gallery and YouTube video (when provided)
  • Direct contact — WhatsApp, KakaoTalk, Facebook Messenger with vehicle context
  • Member offer panel — submit and review your purchase interest (signed-in members)

04 · Transparent trading system
Process integrity

Clear offers. Clear next steps.

Messaging is fast. The platform also provides structured tools so price discussions and follow-up stay organized.

1. Inquire

Ask via WhatsApp, KakaoTalk, or Messenger. Listing buttons attach vehicle identity for you.

2. Offer

Members can submit a purchase offer with amount and currency on the listing page.

3. Track

Open My offers to review open / closed interest in one place.

Live Auction (members)

Live Auction listings are available to registered members. Guests see a clear access message with Login / Register options. Once signed in, you can review auction units and participate through the same offer workflow where enabled.

Communication Multiple official channels — WhatsApp, KakaoTalk, Messenger, email — with the same contact team.
Commercial path How to buy explains price check → invoice currency → deposit → shipment release.

05 · How to buy
Guidance system

Four steps to shipment

Follow this sequence for a smooth close. Details also appear on the website under How to buy.

Step 01 · Price

Receive price

Check pricing via WhatsApp (or other official chat channels) for the specific unit.

Transport, taxes, and export declaration fees are charged separately.

Step 02 · Invoice

Generate invoice

Choose currency for documents:

KRW — tax invoice / export declaration · USD — export declaration

Step 03 · Payment

Deposit

Keep the agreed deposit date. Shipment is arranged only after deposit confirmation.

Step 04 · Release

Shipment

Buyer is responsible for shipping costs. Arrange transport yourself, or share address/contact for assistance.

Urgent matters For time-sensitive questions, prefer KakaoTalk or Facebook Messenger so the team can respond quickly during business hours.
